Frequently Asked Questions

Open OlaqVerify and select the sign-up option. Enter the requested information and complete the registration process.
Open your wallet and select Add Money. Follow the payment instructions shown there. After submitting your payment reference, your payment can be reviewed by the platform.
Payments may remain pending while they are being reviewed. Keep your transaction/reference details available if you need to contact support.
Log into your OlaqVerify account and open the Orders section to view your order information and available status updates.
Tell support what happened and include relevant information such as your account email, order reference or payment reference. Never send your password or private security credentials.
Order availability can change. If transactions are temporarily paused, OlaqVerify will display a notice on the platform until ordering is reopened.
